About

I help organisations make sense of complex domains and turn them into clearer products, systems, and decisions. My work sits at the intersection of aviation, data, digital infrastructure, and research.

Alongside my role building Europejska Akademia Lotnictwa, I also work independently on consulting projects that involve product thinking, market insight, digital tooling, and industry research.

What I do

Aviation consulting

Research, structuring, and advisory work across aviation training, maintenance, operations, and market development. This includes ecosystem mapping, opportunity discovery, and industry insight gathered through direct participation in aviation events and networks.

Data products

I help shape data products that turn messy operational information into something an organisation can actually use. My background includes data product ownership, business intelligence, measurement frameworks, analytics infrastructure, and data-informed decision systems.

Product building

I support early product definition, MVP shaping, workflow design, and product strategy. This is especially useful when a company has a complex problem, fragmented inputs, and needs a practical product direction rather than a large transformation programme.

Digital systems and research

I work on digital tooling, research, structured analysis, and decision support for organisations operating in technical or high-trust environments.

Focus areas

  • Aviation and aerospace
  • Data products and analytics
  • Product creation and MVP thinking
  • Digital infrastructure and workflows
  • Strategic research and market insight
  • Cross-border operational projects in Europe

Background

My experience spans aviation, consulting, product, analytics, and operations. It includes building data functions from scratch, leading product ownership in data-heavy organisations, developing digital maintenance tooling, and structuring aviation-related initiatives in Europe.

That mix is useful for projects where technical detail, product thinking, and business reality all need to come together.
